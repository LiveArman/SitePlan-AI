The Problem

Great ideas often start with incomplete requirements.

People know what they want to build, but they often do not know all the decisions required to turn that idea into a reliable website or application.

Missing Requirements

Important features and project decisions can easily be forgotten before development begins.

Technical Complexity

Non-technical users often struggle to understand technology, architecture and integration choices.

AI Needs Better Context

AI coding systems become more useful when they receive clear, structured project requirements.

The Vision

A planning layer for the age of AI-assisted development.

AI can dramatically accelerate software development. But before software can be built effectively, someone still needs to understand what should actually be built.

SitePlan AI aims to become that planning layer between human ideas and AI-assisted software development.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SitePlan AI?

SitePlan AI is an AI-powered platform designed to help users discover website requirements, make planning decisions and generate structured project specifications before development.

Is SitePlan AI only for developers?

No. The platform is intended to support both technical and non-technical users. A major goal is to make project planning easier for people who have ideas but do not have deep technical knowledge.

How does AI fit into the product?

AI can help interpret project descriptions, explain technical choices, provide recommendations and identify potential gaps in a project's requirements.

Are you looking for developers?

Yes. The project is open to conversations with developers, technical contributors, designers and other people interested in helping build an early-stage AI product.

Are you looking for investment?

Yes. SitePlan AI is open to conversations with suitable investors and strategic partners who are interested in the product vision and the future of AI-assisted software development.
